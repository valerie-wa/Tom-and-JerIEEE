Micromouse using
- ESP32s3 sense xiao microcontroller
- 12V motor + encoder
- TB6612FNG motor driver
- 6 pairs of Infrared Emitters/Recievers
- Analog to Digital Converter
- GPIO Expander
- 2 3.7V Lipo Batteries
- 2 Ball Bearings
- 2 Slide Switches
- 2 Wheels
- Inertial Measuring Unit
- 5V Regulator for microcontroller
- 12V Step up Regulator for Motor
- And assorted LEDS/resistors/capacitors/transistors
